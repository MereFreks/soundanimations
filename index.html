Bachelor's Thesis Research

Sound Effects
Synthesis
in Digital Animation

My name is Domantas Juzikis, I am a Multimedia Design fourth year student (group MKDfu-22), Vilnius TECH university. This experiment is part of my bachelor's thesis Sound Effects Synthesis in Digital Animation. In this study, I am exploring how different sound design approaches influence the way people perceive animated scenes.

Participants will view three versions of the same animated sequence: a baseline with no sound, one featuring AI-generated audio, and one created via traditional (Foley) sound design. The labeling remains neutral to ensure an unbiased evaluation.

The research aims to identify how these methods impact emotional engagement, realism, and audiovisual coherence, identifying the unique strengths of both Foley practices and emerging AI tools.

After viewing, you will complete a brief questionnaire. Your participation is anonymous and submitted responses are used strictly for academic purposes.
